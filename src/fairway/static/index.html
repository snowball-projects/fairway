    How This Works

    Committed origins select courses within a straight-line radius. They do not calculate driving times.

    Driving-time comparison is not connected in this public release. Browse course facts and verify booking with the operator.

    Addresses are sent to Photon only when you press Enter or leave the field. Manually entered coordinates stay in your browser.

    Typed course and tee fields appear where documented. Typed optional observations retain their own scope, dates and conditions. Quality/star ratings and live tee-time availability are unknown. Software by snowball.
