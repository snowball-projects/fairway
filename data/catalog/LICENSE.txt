ODbL does not replace rights in individual contents. Each source keeps its own
license, credit, scope and change notice in metadata.json (source_licenses) and
in fact_sources / the observation's source envelope. Utah UGRC attributes retain
CC-BY-4.0 attribution and adaptation notices. Scoped City of Moorhead facts retain
the recorded CC-BY-SA-3.0-Unported notice. Source license null means no license is
recorded for that individual source; it is not a grant of rights over its page.
The inherited catalog records bare facts and explicitly licensed attributes;
private source prose, evidence, notes, controls and saved pages are not included.
No permission to reuse third-party writing or artwork is asserted.

The CSV files are maintained source files. GeoJSON and compressed browser JSON
are reproducible projections. Unknown check dates remain null. Historical-catalog
citations identify the exact earlier published catalog when an upstream field
binding is unresolved; they are not newly verified primary-source citations.
Coordinates retain their recorded kinds. Course map points inherited from their
facility do not establish course entrances. Missing geometry remains null.
Phone/email fields and embedded contact values have been removed. Designer names
and their credits have been retained with the owner's explicit approval.
